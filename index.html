THE SUPPLY DESK

Who we can approach.

These are real Indian wholesale suppliers/manufacturers with published B2B routes. They are sourcing leads, not confirmed THREADCRAFT partners.

JEWELLERY · WHOLESALE

Tohfa Jewellery

Mumbai-based B2B supplier for imitation jewellery, 92.5 silver jewellery and hair accessories. Its site says it serves retailers and online sellers across India. Ask for catalogue, current MOQ, sample policy and reseller terms.

JEWELLERY · MANUFACTURER

Sasitrends

Fashion jewellery manufacturer and wholesale programme for online sellers and boutiques. Published minimum wholesale order: ₹5,000. Confirm GST, shipping, returns and current catalogue before ordering.

OM Enterprises

Noida-based wholesale partner for fashion jewellery and handbags, with low-MOQ and private-label options stated on its site. Ask for exact MOQ, landed price and sample images.

HANDBAGS · WHOLESALE

Shoppers Bazaar

Indian B2B handbag supplier for retailers, boutiques and online resellers. Its site advertises factory-direct wholesale pricing; verify current rates, minimum quantities and quality before buying.

KUNDAN & FASHION JEWELLERY

Sirini Jewellery

Mumbai manufacturer offering wholesale enquiries to boutiques and resellers. It says there is no fixed MOQ; terms are discussed based on products and expected quantities.

Before any order: request a sample, invoice, GST details, written MOQ, dispatch timeline, return/damage policy, product material details and permission to use their product photos/brand names.
